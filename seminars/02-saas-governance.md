# Seminar 2 — SaaS security and security governance

[Back to seminars](README.md) · [Home](../README.md)

**Syllabus section:** SaaS Security; Security Governance
**Slides:** 20-22

## Focus
How a SaaS provider and a tenant run security as a process, not a product.

## Include
- How SaaS risk differs from IaaS
- People and security management
- Governance, policies, standards, guidelines
- Awareness and training
- Third-party / vendor risk

## Exclude
Detailed IAM (seminar 8); WAF (seminar 9).

## Example
Hospital buys a SaaS EHR: contract clauses, DPO role, vendor questionnaire.

## Current status
SOC 2 and ISO 27017 / 27018 in SaaS contracts.

Rittinghouse Ch. 6; Mather Ch. 6.
